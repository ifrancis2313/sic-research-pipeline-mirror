# Require a four-person evidence gate for every factor change

SIC treats its factor methodology as a durable investment process, not a collection of tunable settings. Adding, removing, or materially changing a factor's definition, formula, direction, or threshold may affect every subsequent research brief, fit card, and sizing recommendation.

Therefore the only path to a live factor change is a substantive research report and thesis brought to the PM, Board Chair, Lead Analyst, and Faculty Advisor. All four must validate and approve the proposal before it changes the live factor registry or the weekly research queue. The report must explain the theory, exact proposed definition, data and point-in-time treatment, empirical validation, limitations, and expected portfolio effect. An active pitch is never sufficient reason to alter a factor; proposed changes remain outside the live process until this gate is complete.

## Alternatives considered

Single-approver authority — letting the PM alone amend a factor — was rejected: it would let one person's judgment, even in good faith, reshape every downstream research brief and sizing recommendation the whole club relies on, which is exactly the failure mode this ADR's own second sentence warns against (an active pitch driving an ad hoc change). No formal gate at all, with factors just edited in code as needed, was also rejected: it would make methodology changes untraceable after the fact, breaking the same audit trail this codebase enforces everywhere else a decision affects capital (`FactorChangeProposal`'s bound evidence record, `ExceptionRecord`'s exact-violation binding). Four required signers rather than a numeric majority was chosen for role diversity, not headcount: PM (operational authority), Board Chair (governance authority), Lead Analyst (technical review), and Faculty Advisor (external perspective) means no single interest group — student leadership, technical staff, or external oversight — can independently drive a change the others haven't actually validated.
